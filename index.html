How Tsumugi Works

A few terms you'll see around the app.

🧵

Weave — paste a few words and hit "Weave Context" to build sentences from only those words.

🗂️

The Vault — every word you weave is saved here, so you can reuse it later with one tap.

📘

The Blueprint — each word gets its own breakdown: readings, meaning, and a mnemonic.

🧭

Follow the thread — click any word inside a sentence to jump straight back to its Blueprint.
